# ANLEYLAB Design Principles

1. Offline First: All content must be accessible without network dependency.
2. Fast by Default: Cold starts under 2s, chapter switches under 50ms.
3. Accessibility Matters: Scalable fonts, clear contrast, and intuitive navigation.
4. Minimal UI: Remove distractions; focus on the Word.
5. Catholic Accuracy: Adherence to the 73-book canon and authorized text.
6. Beautiful Typography: Prioritize readability and legible Ethiopic rendering.
7. Performance Before Features: Optimization is a feature.
8. Privacy by Design: No data leaves the device unless explicitly authorized.
9. Consistency Everywhere: Uniform spacing, iconography, and interactions.
10. Long-term Maintainability: Clean, decoupled, and well-documented code.
